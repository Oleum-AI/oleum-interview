# Benchmark Questions

## Easy — answerable from the data alone

How many products are in the catalog, and how many are currently active? — **600 total, 542 active**

Which product category has the most products? — **Women's Clothing (68)**

How many carriers are active? — **9**

## Medium — fails cold; a doc-search tool flips it to reliably correct

What is our order fill rate? — **77.57%**

How many active enterprise customers do we have? — **265**

How many orders were placed through the marketplace channel? — **3,795**

## Hard — doc-search necessary but not sufficient; must reconcile the docs with the data

What was net revenue after returns for 2024? — **$9,934,476.10**

What was total recognized revenue for 2024? — **$10,382,697.48**

How many active inventory positions had zero outbound movement in the trailing 90 days? — **676**
